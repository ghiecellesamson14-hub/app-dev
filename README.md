# The Green Mile

**The Green Mile** is a 1999 American *fantasy drama* film written and directed by Frank Darabont, adapted from Stephen King's 1996 serialized novel of the same name.

> "I'm tired, boss. Tired of bein' on the road, lonely as a sparrow in the rain." — John Coffey

## Main Cast & Characters

4. **Doug Hutchison** as Percy Wetmore

## Notable Themes & Elements

- **Supernatural Healing:** John Coffey possesses a mysterious, miraculous gift to absorb illness and pain.
- **Injustice & Empathy:** Despite being wrongly convicted, Coffey shows profound compassion toward the guards and fellow inmates.
- **Memorable Props:** Mr. Jingles, the clever circus mouse adopted by inmate Eduard Delacroix.
- ~~Happy Endings~~ — The film is famous for its bittersweet and deeply emotional conclusion.
